# Pass 3, 2026-11-19: overheating as a regime, longer horizons, time in state, TD Sequential

Pre-registered in PREREG3.md (with its two dated amendments) before any cell was scored. Harness: harness/harness2.py
(3D and 4H bars added), harness/harness3.py (TD Sequential, days-in-state, exits), harness/score3.py, harness/base_rates.py.
Results: ../../results/outside_2026-11-19/ (R1-R5 JSON, reports, base_rates.json, refute/). Every candidate that
looked like something was handed to an independent refuter with the pre-registered refutations plus a simpler-control
check; those scripts and outputs are under results/outside_2026-11-19/refute/.

## The question
Pass 2 found that crowded-long readings (funding, open interest, basis at their highs) were followed by SHALLOWER
drawdowns at 10-30 days in 2021-25. The owner's point: a consistent effect in either direction is information. So:
does it hold at 30-90 days as a risk-on reading (R1); does risk rise with time spent in the crowded state (R2); is
the exit from the state where the drawdown is (R3); does TD Sequential carry information on 1H, 4H, 1D, 3D (R4);
and do the pass-1/2 near misses work at 30-90 days (R5)?

## Verdict
Nothing passed the pre-registered rules and survived refutation. About 420 daily/3D cell-horizons and 150
exploratory hourly rows were scored; one cell passed the rule (ETH daily TD9 buy setup, 10-day drawdown) and is
consistent with chance. The findings that looked like a family all reduce to a simpler explanation. Details:

### R1. Overheating at 30-90 days
- Risk-on hypothesis (crowded funding/OI/basis -> shallower drawdowns, positive excess): train only. BTC funding decile
  W=30/60/90 +3.5/+5.3/+7.5 pts (t 2.6-2.8), ETH +5.3/+7.6/+9.7 (t 3.2-3.6); validation ETH +3.5/+6.4/+5.5 (t ~2),
  BTC ~0; 2026 ETH flips to deeper (-5.9 pts at W=90). 1-4 episodes per period. Not a reading.
- USD open interest at its highs -> weak 30-90 day returns (BTC and ETH, train and validation agreeing in sign) was the
  one family-level pattern. It does not survive: the OI series are USD notional with corr(log OI, log price)
  0.87-0.92 for BTC, and 79-83% of OI-top days are price-top days. The price-only control reproduces the validation
  effect (ETH price top: -6.9/-19.9/-32.5% at 30/60/90, the Dec-2024 top); coin-denominated OI (OI/price) top is
  strong in train only (one 2021-10..2022-12 episode) and ~0 after. The refuter's OLS on [price-top, coin-OI-top]
  leaves OI insignificant in validation. Fast 30-day OI build-ups carry no 30-90 day information either.
- A harness flaw found by that refuter and fixed the same day: harness2.score_flag compared flagged bars against ALL
  bars of the period with a forward return, so a series that starts in 2021 (OI, basis) was benchmarked against the
  2019-21 bull run; train 'excess' for late-starting series was too negative by 5-20 points at 60-90 days. The
  baseline is now the bars where the signal is defined. R1/R2/R3/R5 were re-scored (results3 JSON are the corrected
  numbers; the pre-fix report is kept as REPORT_R1_R2_R3_R5.txt beside REPORT_R1_R2_R3_R5_fixed_baseline.txt).
  After the fix: ETH perp_oi 0.8 train -2.3/-4.2/-3.3% (was -8.8/-19.0/-27.1); BTC perp_oi 0.9 -5.8/-10.9/-10.7
  (was -10.4/-21.5/-27.7); basis top train turns positive. The pass-2 cells on late-starting series are being re-run
  with the fixed harness; pass-2 verdicts were all rejections/insufficient and the fix only moves train numbers for
  OI, basis, put/call and options-OI cells, so no pass-2 conclusion is expected to change (addendum below once run).
- BTC long/short ratio top -> weaker returns and deeper drawdowns in all three periods (30-90 days: excess
  -2.5/-4.2/-5.1 at 30 days after the fix; W=30 mdd -3.4/-1.1/-4.8 pts, crash lift 1.4-1.8; 10/4/3 episodes). Refuted:
  (a) the dashboard lsr_BTC series stepped up in level in 2021 (mean 1.35 -> 2.11, std 0.29 -> 0.85), so the trailing
  percentile flagged two-thirds of 2021H1; 54% of train top-quintile days are in 2021 and the train result is two 2021
  clusters sitting on the May-2021 crash and the 2022 bear; train ex-2021 is flat. (b) Since mid-2024 a top reading is,
  80-90% of the time, a day on which BTC already fell over the prior 30 days (Spearman -0.70 with the trailing 30-day
  return); a trailing-30d-return bottom-quintile control reproduces the train and validation 30-day excess
  (-5.6/-4.1% vs -4.2/-4.2%) with more episodes. (c) Thresholds 0.7, 0.75 and 0.95 break the three-period agreement in
  test. (d) No episode-level sign test reaches p < 0.1. The Overview 'positioning watch' card that pass 2 justified is
  reworded in the dashboard PR: context, not a watch level.

### R2. Time in the crowded state
All 12 series-asset verdicts insufficient (the longest bucket has 1-7 episodes). The suggestive ETH pattern (validation
>60-day high-OI bucket: mdd -7.5 pts, t -2.9) is three terminal stretches of long runs that ended in the Aug-2024,
Feb-2025 and Oct-2025 crashes. Refuted: price-at-highs duration reproduces it (hysteresis -10.1 vs OI -8.1) and absorbs
the BTC version entirely (-2.8 -> -0.7); coin-OI duration shows nothing (-0.6); train shows nothing at any bucket edge;
a USD-OI run ends precisely when price drops, so 'late in the run' is 'just before the drop' by construction.

### R3. Exit from the crowded state
Too rare to score as defined (20 consecutive days above the 80th percentile): 6 funding exits on BTC in seven years,
0-14 for the others; the post-hoc hysteresis variant adds a few. Nothing showable.

### R4. TD Sequential (closes only; perfected on closes; countdown closes vs close 2 bars earlier)
- Implementation verified by an independent re-implementation from the written definition: identical on every bar of
  every scored frame (1D, 3D x3 anchors, 4H, 1H), identical on 200 random walks. One deviation in a mode score3 never
  uses (countdown update skipped on NaN bars). Caveat from that review: without highs/lows, the price-flip prerequisite,
  the bar-13 qualifier and TDST cancellation, this is the stated definition, not the full published indicator.
- Frequency: a TD9 is not rare under a martingale. Random walk: 14 buy9 per 1000 bars; BTC daily 10.8, ETH 10.1.
- Returns: 0 of 48 return cells pass; the DeMark-signed mean t by period is -0.55/+0.38/+0.27 (1D), ~0 on 3D, 4H, 1H.
  Setup-count curves (what happens at count 7, 8, 9) are train-only on BTC (late sell counts +3.2% at 10 days, t 2.6 in
  train; -1.1% validation, -1.7% test) and flip in 2026 on ETH.
- The one rule pass: ETH TD9 buy, W=10 drawdown, validation +9.2 pts t 4.9. Four validation completions (2024-08-09,
  2025-02-11, 2025-06-22, 2025-09-25 entries), two of them carry it; a random-date placebo passes the full rule 2.8%
  of the time (about 7 passes expected across ~250 risk cell-windows, 1 observed); test +0.9 pts (t 0.5) with the sign
  flipping at W=20/30 and at lag 0; BTC does not confirm; the effect is not specific to bar 9 (present at 8 and 10+).
- The opposite-to-book regularity (daily buy countdown 13 -> further weakness over 3-5 days; 18 of 24 independent
  BTC/ETH episodes negative, binomial p 0.011) is real as a description of 2019-25 and is NOT explained by trailing
  20/60-day return, momentum or down-close density. It is refuted as a finding: under the 30-episode bar, BTC flips at
  countdown length 12 and 15, the two assets share most episodes (6 independent validation episodes), BTC train depends
  on excluding a +2.7% day-1 bounce, 3D fails on 2 of 3 anchors, and every 2026 completion has positive excess.
- Hourly data: 633 short price gaps in 2024-11..2025-07 (36% of hours in Dec-2024..Mar-2025). Counting over available
  bars is unbiased in rate and direction but the individual 1H flags inside that window are wrong about a third of the
  time; the clean treatment is to mask that window. Since no 1H/4H cell passed, no conclusion changes.

### R5. Long horizons for the near misses
Crowded-short funding, IV/RV bottom and skew_30 top at 30-90 days all change sign between periods (e.g. ETH funding
bottom h=90 train -35% t -3.0 on 3 episodes, validation +12.7% on 2). Nothing.

## Method gaps recorded by the completeness critic (for the next pre-registration)
1. The period mean as drift control uses in-period future information; use a past-only trailing baseline or the spread
   against unflagged bars inside the signal window. (The signal-window fix above addresses the late-start part only.)
2. Newey-West t at lag h on a flag that occupies 2-6 clusters is not interpretable; report episode-level means and a
   t on n_episodes or a cluster bootstrap.
3. A state that spans a period boundary is counted as a fresh episode in both periods (lsr top 2024-07-01 and
   2026-01-01; funding top 2024-07-01 and 2026-01-02); assign a cluster to the period of its first bar.
4. No level percentile of open interest is clean: USD OI is price, coin OI peaked in the 2022 bear. Use changes
   (coin-OI 7-day change) or residuals to price, never the level.
5. Percentile ties: 23-41% of funding days sit exactly at the 0.01% Binance default, so the 'washed' state is measured
   on non-default days only, and 'top quintile' in 2025-26 means a third of the absolute leverage of 2021.
6. Units: daily2.pkl perp_funding_* equals the dashboard funding column (percent per 8h), not a fraction; hourly.pkl
   btc_funding is perp_funding x 100. Percentile cells are unaffected; carry uses the dashboard column via
   harness.funding (divides by 100), which is correct. base_rates.py no longer mixes the two scales.
7. Probable liquidation coverage break: liq_BTC / oi_BTC yearly medians 0.0018 (2022), 0.0019, 0.0024, 0.0013 (2025),
   0.0028 (2026) while volatility fell; liq_BTC median doubled 2025 -> 2026 as oi_BTC fell. Treat any liq-based reading
   as a data break until checked (backlog item D1).
8. The three periods are three regimes (2020-21 bull plus 2022 bear; 2024H2-25 bull-to-chop; 10.5 months of low vol
   with funding at a third of the default and a longest crowded run of 5 days). 'Same sign in all three' is three
   draws.

## Proposed next pre-registration (PREREG4, not scored; episode counts estimated by the critic)
- A1. Hazard of observable de-leveraging events (coin-OI 3-day drop <= -10%, DVOL 5-day spike top decile, funding reset
  from >= 0.8 to < 0.5) within 10/20 days of a crowded-funding state vs unconditional; control: trailing 20-day
  realized vol quintile. The mechanism behind 'overheating', tested without a price target.
- A2. Price x coin-OI 7-day quadrants (leveraged rally / long flush / short cover / new shorts) on 10-20 day excess
  and drawdown; 24-38 episodes; control: the 7-day price return alone must not explain it (conditioned lift rule).
- A3. Crowded funding with coin OI building vs leaving; control: A2 quadrants and USD-OI version.
- A5. Funding-state transition matrix (crowded/neutral/washed at 7 days) per regime as a base rate: P(still crowded
  in 7 days) BTC 0.67 / 0.53 / 0.27 by period, vs occupancy 0.39 / 0.26 / 0.19; 2026 differs in persistence.
- A6. Funding reset while price holds (healthy cooling) vs with a 5-day drop (flush); 32-36 episodes.
- A4 (exploratory, below the bar): breadth of alt funding crowding -> alt/BTC relative return, with the 0.01-default

## Addendum, 2026-11-19 evening: pass 2 re-run with the signal-window baseline
H1-H4 of PREREG2 were re-scored with the fixed harness2 (results compared file by file against the pre-fix JSON).
196 result files have identical verdicts; 109 changed wording (a cell moving between 'rejected: sign differs',
'rejected: validation t below 2.5' and 'insufficient', or an episode count moving by a few) with no verdict moving to
PASS except one: ETH 25d risk reversal 30d, top decile -> up at 3 and 5 days (validation excess +3.1/+3.9%, t 2.6, 7
episodes; train +0.2%, test +0.6/+1.3%). Pre-fix it was 'rejected: validation t 1.33'. The validation baseline moved
because 22 validation days have no risk-reversal value (two Laevitas gaps, 2024-07-10 and 2024-10-10) and those days
happened to carry a +2.4% mean 5-day return. Refuted on the spot (results/outside_2026-11-19/refute/rr30_top_eth_after_fix.txt):
thresholds 0.8 and 0.85 flip the train sign, 0.95 falls to t 2.48, dropping the best three validation episodes turns
the episode-level mean from +4.3% to -0.9% (one week, 2025-07-14, contributes +18.9%), BTC does not confirm (validation
t 1.7), ex-2020 train turns negative, and the mirror cell (ETH skew_30 bottom) has train negative. A pass at the 2.5
boundary on 7 episodes in one period: noise. Pass-2 conclusions stand.

## Addendum 2, 2026-11-19 late: base-rate table rebuilt on the dashboard series; the owner's alt-froth reading
A review of the State tab found that the live fifth (computed on data.json columns) and the base-rate table (built on
the Laevitas tenors) agreed on the fifth only 35-73% of days for funding, open interest, skew and IV/RV. The table is
now built on the same dashboard columns the page shows (base_rates.py readings()), so the two cannot disagree; the
page percentile was also aligned with the panel's rolling rank (share of the previous 364 days at or below the value).

Owner's hypothesis, pre-registered in PREREG_ALTFROTH.md and scored once (results/outside_2026-11-19/altfroth_report.txt):
"alt OI above BTC OI with large-alt funding above a level". In the 20-coin dashboard universe alts alone never exceed
BTC open interest (0.31-0.85x); alts plus ETH do on 33% of days. Large-alt funding above the 1 bp default (B) was on 16%
of days: in 2022-09..2024-06 BTC's next 20-30 days were weaker (-2.8/-4.5%, t -1.3/-1.4) with deeper drawdowns
(-1.4/-2.6/-3.6 pts, t -1.9 to -2.1, crash lift 1.4-1.55, 9-17 episodes) while the alts outperformed BTC (+4.4/+8.3/+12.3%,
t 3.5/2.7/2.2: froth continuing); one episode (22 days, positive) since mid-2024 and never on in 2026. Both conditions
(froth) on 7% of days in 27 runs of median 2 days, last on 2024-12-09: BTC 30d -3.7% on 5 train episodes, one validation
episode, nothing in test. Verdict: a real, observable regime that is mostly a 2023 to early-2024 phenomenon here and
cannot be checked out of sample on this universe; shown on the State tab as a composite reading with its on-record and
that caveat, not as a signal. A broader all-venue alt universe (Hermes's top-100 stores) is the natural next check.
