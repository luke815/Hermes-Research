periods with validation |t| >= 2.5 and 20 episodes. The decile calibrations of the continuous readings flip sign between

equity 20-day returns are negative in train and 2026 (mean reversion into BTC), not continuation. The two readings with

-3%; 17 episodes), and an S&P 20-day washout (bottom fifth) -> +1/+4/+8% in the three periods (20 episodes, validation

## Trend
BTC's own 20-day return and distance from the 50-day mean have monotone 30-day calibration in train (+0.67, +0.78) and
validation (+0.70, +0.61), i.e. continuation worked in 2020-2025, and flip in 2026 (-0.20, -0.25; 60-day return -0.77).
The ret20-top cell: +3.4 / +2.2 / -2.6% at 30 days; sign differs in test. This is the same picture as Hermes's heat
(a trend composite): calibrated 2021-25, opposite in 2026. 2026 has been a mean-reverting, low-volatility regime in
which neither positioning, flows, macro nor trend has carried the sign it had before.

## What this means for the plan
P1/P2 are done for the free inputs; the answer is that at the daily-close, 10-60-day horizon with fifth-of-year states,
none of these single inputs times BTC across regimes. The remaining honest avenues: (1) spot ETF flows (needs a key),
(2) regime-conditional rules tested as such (trend in trending regimes, reversal in chop; the regime must be
identified without hindsight), (3) the cross-section (which alts are strong or weak), where momentum and reversal have
more support than BTC timing, on Hermes's point-in-time top-100 universe. The dashboard carries the five macro readings
as context tiles with the same base-rate treatment as the rest.
